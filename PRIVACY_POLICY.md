# Privacy Policy for Istanbuller

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2025

## Introduction

Istanbuller ("we", "our", or "us") is a location-based tourist guide application for Istanbul. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **Purpose:** We collect your device's location data to provide location-based tourist recommendations and nearby attractions in Istanbul.
- **Type:** Precise location (GPS coordinates)
- **Usage:** Location data is used only while the app is in use to determine your current district and show relevant nearby attractions.
- **Storage:** Location data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

### Language Preference
- We store your selected language preference locally on your device to provide content in your preferred language.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Determine your current location within Istanbul
- Show nearby tourist attractions and points of interest
- Provide district-specific recommendations
- Display content in your preferred language

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device
- We do not transmit your location data to external servers
- We do not share your information with third parties
- No personal identification information is collected or stored

## Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services that may collect information:
- **Expo Framework:** Used for app development and updates
- **Google Play Services:** Required for location services on Android devices

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Deny location permissions (though this will limit app functionality)
- Clear app data at any time through your device settings
- Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

## Location Permissions

- The app requests location permissions to function properly
- You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings
- Without location access, you can still manually browse districts and attractions

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
